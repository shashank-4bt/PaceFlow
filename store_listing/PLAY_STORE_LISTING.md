## Full description (4000 characters max)

```
PaceFlow is a premium walking tracker built for people who want clarity, not clutter. Record every walk with accurate GPS routes, live stats, and beautiful summaries — offline-first, so your progress is never lost when signal drops.

EVERY STEP HAS A STORY
Whether you walk for fitness, mindfulness, or daily commute steps, PaceFlow turns your movement into meaningful data you can trust and share.

KEY FEATURES

• Live GPS tracking — See your route on the map as you walk
• Real-time stats — Distance, duration, pace, and calories at a glance
• Background tracking — Continue recording when the screen is off (with your permission)
• Offline-first — Walks save locally and sync when you are back online
• Walk history — Review completed routes, metrics, and summaries
• Goals & streaks — Stay motivated with daily and weekly targets (rolling out)
• Achievements — Unlock badges as you hit milestones (rolling out)
• Dark, premium UI — Designed for outdoor readability day and night
• Google Sign-In — Fast, secure account setup
• Privacy controls — Your routes belong to you

BUILT FOR WALKERS
PaceFlow focuses on walking — not running marathons or gym workouts. Our metrics engine filters GPS noise, estimates calories from your pace and weight, and encodes efficient route polylines for fast sync.

SMART & RELIABLE
• GPS filtering reduces jitter and impossible speed jumps
• Step counter integration where your device supports it
• Foreground service keeps tracking stable on modern Android
• Firebase sync across devices when you sign in

PERMISSIONS EXPLAINED
PaceFlow requests location (including background), notifications, activity recognition, and network access solely to track walks, show maps, remind you to move, and sync your data. We do not sell your personal information.

SAFETY REMINDER
Always stay aware of your surroundings. PaceFlow is not a medical device. Consult a doctor before starting a new exercise program.

Download PaceFlow and make every walk count.
```
